How Tingle works

  1. Enter your date of birth and pick a display name.
  2. Allow your camera and mic, and preview yourself.
  3. We match you with one random person who's also ready.
  4. Talk as long as you both want — end the call whenever you like.
  5. Block or report anytime. Nothing is recorded.
